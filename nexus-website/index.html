Do you love to sing?
Whether you have formal singing experience and musical training, or you just can't help singing in the shower, we'd love to hear your voice!

Initial auditions consist of a 30 second-1 minute solo of your choice performed without accompaniment, a range test, and an aural skills assessment.

We hold auditions once a year, next date TBA.

Currently rehearsing Sundays 6-8pm at The Arts Hub in Lafayette.
Music Director: Brandon Warren

Before directing Nexus Acapella, Brandon Warren directed the collegiate acapella group On That Note at CU Boulder. He is a talented and knowledgeable musician with extensive experience in musical theater, vocal arranging and directing, and transcribing pieces that mortal ears simply couldn't parse. His current day job is teaching high school mathematics, and he is also an avid player of dungeons and dragons and Clone Hero (which is Guitar Hero but with WAY harder levels). His leadership style is warm and inclusive, and he seeks to bring out the unique talent of every singer in the group.

After graduating from CU Boulder, a group of alumni from collegiate acapella group On That Note wanted to start a new acapella group that was open to anyone, not just college students. After reaching out to connections in the local performing arts community, as well as putting out a social media blast on every platform imaginable (hello, redditors!), Nexus Acapella was assembled in 2024. Nexus performs mostly original arrangements of contemporary music genres sourced from group members.
