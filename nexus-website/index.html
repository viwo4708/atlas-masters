Colorado's Community Acapella Group

Nexus Acapella strives to bring together singers of different ages, backgrounds, and genders. We perform mostly original arrangements sourced from group members, covering a wide range of genres with a focus on contemporary popular music. We hold social events, make connections outside of rehearsals, and share a love of singing and musical excellence.


We hope to see you at one of our concerts, or at auditions!


Follow us on Instagram @nexusacapella to keep up with auditions, concert dates and tickets, and more!
